# Credits and licences

Everything in the Nafaa Meta ad set is either made by Nafaa or used under an open licence. No stock photos, no AI-generated images and no AI-generated people are used.

## Screens

All app screens are real captures of the Nafaa web app (`apps/web`) running locally against local demo shops (`demo-*@nafaa.test`). The demo shops were created and filled only through the local Nafaa API: products, customers, khata entries, orders and sales were entered the same way a shop would enter them. Shop names contain "Demo", phone numbers are `0300000xxxx` placeholders, and customer names are invented. No real shop's data appears.

- Source files: `brand/ads/source/screens/<industry>/`
- Owner: Nafaa. Free to use in Nafaa's own advertising.
